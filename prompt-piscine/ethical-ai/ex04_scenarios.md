Using AI fairly today means using it as a support tool rather than a substitute for understanding. When I review its suggestions, question its decisions, and make sure I truly grasp the underlying concepts, I strengthen my own problem-solving ability. This prepares me for real-world challenges, like explaining system designs in interviews, debugging production issues without assistance, or learning brand-new technologies—because I am not dependent on AI to think for me. Instead, I build confidence, technical depth, and adaptability, ensuring I can perform effectively even when AI is unavailable or incomplete.
